One hand can change your whole run.

Tensho is a single-player mahjong roguelike about building powerful tile combinations and finding clever ways to score. Shape your hand, choose your upgrades, and take on rising targets across a run of increasingly demanding rounds.

BUILD YOUR HAND
Arrange tiles into scoring combinations, weigh your next play, and decide when to redraw. Learn mahjong-inspired patterns, then put them to work in a strategy of your own.

BEND THE RULES
Collect Decrees that change how you play. Combine scoring bonuses, new opportunities, and unusual effects to turn an ordinary hand into the heart of a powerful build.

MAKE EVERY CHOICE COUNT
Visit the shop between rounds. Spend your gold on upgrades and consumables, plan for the next challenge, and adapt when a boss changes the rules.

GROW BEYOND ONE RUN
Earn achievements and unlock new rewards. Explore different tables and discover combinations that make you want to try one more run.

LEARN AS YOU PLAY
Contextual guidance and an in-game Codex help you understand the tiles, patterns, and possibilities. Follow a familiar strategy or experiment with something unexpected.

Ivory tiles, illustrated scrolls, and a richly colored tabletop bring every decision to life.

Build the hand. Find the combo. Make the ascent.
