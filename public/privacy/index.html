Privacy Policy 天翔

Tensho (天翔) Mahjong roguelike · Last updated 30 October 2026

  • You don't need an account to play, and Tensho collects no analytics.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. We run no server that receives them.
  • The website shows no ads.
  • The iPhone app shows an occasional full-screen ad from Google AdMob between rounds. Google collects some device data to serve those ads, as described below.

Who we are

Tensho is an independent game made by Evgeny Vinnik, who is responsible for the information described here. It is available on the web at tensho.app and as an iPhone app. For questions about this policy, see Contact.

Your game data

Tensho saves your runs, unlocks, achievements, statistics and settings (such as language and sound) so you can pick up where you left off. This data:

  • is stored only on your device: in your browser's local storage on the website, and in the app's own storage on iPhone. The iPhone app keeps a second copy in its private settings storage, because iOS can clear web storage when space runs low;
  • is never sent to us or to anyone else, and contains no name, email or contact details;
  • is included in your device's own backups (for example an iCloud backup of your iPhone) if you use them.

The website also stores the game's files in your browser so it can be played offline. Tensho sets no cookies.

The website

The website is a set of static files and loads nothing from other companies: no ads, no analytics, no social widgets and no third-party fonts. Like any website, the service that hosts it receives technical details with each request, such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep them briefly in server logs for security and reliability. We don't use those logs to identify or track you.

Ads in the iPhone app

The iPhone app is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. Now and then, between rounds, it shows a full-screen ad that you can close. The ads come from Google and its advertising partners, not from us. To choose, deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (such as your country or city) can be worked out;
  • device details such as model, iOS version, language and time zone;
  • details about the app, and how you interact with the ads you see (for example, views and taps).

Tensho itself never receives this information and does not share your game data with Google. Google handles it under its own policies, including How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Google may process it in the United States and other countries.

Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience with parental guidance. Tensho is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Tracking. The first time you open the app, iOS asks whether Tensho may track you. If you decline, Google can't use your advertising identifier, and the ads you see are less tailored to you. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. The game plays exactly the same either way.
  • Consent in Europe. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent, using Google's consent message, before ads can use your data for personalization. You can accept, decline or choose options partner by partner. Ads may still be shown if you decline, but they won't be personalized. To change your answer later, open Settings › Privacy Choices in the game.
  • Google ad settings. You can review how Google personalizes ads at My Ad Center.
  • Deleting your data. In the game, Settings › Reset All Progress deletes your runs, achievements, statistics and unlocks. Your language, sound and accessibility settings are kept. To delete everything, clear this site's data in your browser, or delete the app from your iPhone.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete on request. For data that Google holds about you, use the tools Google provides through the links above.

We do not sell your personal information. On iPhone, turning off tracking stops your advertising identifier from being used to personalize ads.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Contact

For questions about this policy or about Tensho, open an issue at github.com/evgenyvinnik/tensho-web/issues. Issues there are public, so please don't include personal details.
